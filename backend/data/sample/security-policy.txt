Company Security Policy — Effective January 2025

Authentication and Access
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all production systems, admin consoles, and VPN access.
- SSO via Okta is required for all employees. Local accounts are prohibited except break-glass emergency accounts.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 90 days for systems that do not support SSO.
- Privileged access reviews occur quarterly. Unused accounts are disabled within 48 hours of offboarding.

Data Classification
- Public: marketing materials approved for external release.
- Internal: team wikis, roadmaps, and operational runbooks.
- Confidential: customer data, financial reports, and HR records.
- Restricted: credentials, encryption keys, and security incident details.

Incident Response
- Report suspected incidents to security@company.com or the #security-alerts Slack channel within 1 hour.
- Severity 1 incidents (data breach, production compromise) trigger the on-call security lead and executive notification.
- All production changes during an active incident require approval from the incident commander.

Device and Endpoint Security
- Company-managed laptops must run the approved MDM agent and full-disk encryption.
- Personal devices may not access production systems or Confidential data.
- USB storage devices are blocked by default; exceptions require Security team approval.

Logging and Monitoring
- Production systems retain audit logs for 12 months.
- Failed login attempts, privilege escalations, and data exports are monitored 24/7.

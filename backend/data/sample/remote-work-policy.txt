Remote Work Policy — Human Resources

Eligibility
- Full-time employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) may request a remote work arrangement.
- Roles that require on-site lab access, hardware testing, or in-person customer delivery are not eligible.

Schedule
- Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week with written manager approval.
- At least 2 days per week must be spent in the office for team collaboration, unless a full-remote exception is granted by HR and the department director.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in the employee's local time zone.

Equipment and Workspace
- The company provides a laptop, monitor stipend ($500 annually), and ergonomic chair reimbursement up to $300.
- Employees must have a dedicated workspace with reliable internet (minimum 50 Mbps download).
- Confidential documents must not be visible during video calls or accessible to household members.

Communication Expectations
- Remote employees must be reachable on Slack during working hours and respond within 2 hours.
- Camera-on is encouraged for team meetings but not required for company-wide town halls.

International Remote Work
- Working outside your home country for more than 14 days per year requires HR and Legal approval.
- Tax and payroll implications must be resolved before travel begins.

Review and Revocation
- Remote arrangements are reviewed every 6 months.
- Managers may revoke remote privileges with 2 weeks notice if team performance or collaboration goals are not met.

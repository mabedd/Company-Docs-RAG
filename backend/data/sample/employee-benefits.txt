Employee Benefits Guide — 2025 Plan Year

Time Off
- Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per year, accrued monthly.
- 10 paid sick days per year, separate from vacation balance.
- 12 company holidays annually (list published each November).
- New parents receive 16 weeks paid parental leave (birthing and non-birthing parents).

Health and Wellness
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following start date.
- Company covers 90% of employee medical premiums; 70% for dependents.
- Annual wellness stipend: $600 for gym, therapy, or fitness apps.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides 8 free counseling sessions per year.

Retirement and Financial
- 401(k) plan with 4% company match, fully vested after 1 year of employment.
- Annual performance bonus target: 10–15% of base salary for individual contributors; 15–25% for managers.
- RSU grants are reviewed during annual compensation cycles for employees at Level 4 and above.

Professional Development
- Learning budget: $2,000 per year for courses, conferences, and certifications.
- Employees may take up to 5 paid "learning days" per year for approved training.
- Internal tech talks and lunch-and-learns count toward professional development hours.

Other Perks
- Commuter benefits: pre-tax transit and parking up to IRS limits.
- Home internet reimbursement: $75/month for eligible remote employees.
- Referral bonus: $3,000 for successful engineering hires, $1,500 for other roles, paid after 90 days.

Enrollment
- Benefits elections are made within 30 days of hire or during open enrollment in November.
- Changes outside open enrollment require a qualifying life event.
- Questions: benefits@company.com
